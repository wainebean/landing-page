Our Story

Rooted in
East Africa.

Kenya has been growing world-class coffee for over a century. But for most of that time, it was not owned or consumed by Kenyans.

That's why our founder John Wainaina started Waine Bean & Brew: to foster and uplift the Kenyan coffee culture, one roast at a time. We work across the entire coffee value chain — sourcing, roasting, and serving — so more Kenyans can fall in love with our own coffee.
